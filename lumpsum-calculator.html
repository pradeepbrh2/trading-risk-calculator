Frequently asked questions

How is lumpsum future value calculated?

The estimate compounds the initial investment at the assumed annual rate for the selected period. It is a mathematical projection, not a forecast.

Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?

No. Market-linked investments can rise or fall, and actual returns are uneven rather than a fixed annual rate.

Should I use CAGR or XIRR?

Use CAGR for a single start and end value over a period. Use XIRR when there are multiple cash flows on different dates.
