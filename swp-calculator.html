How an SWP calculator works

A systematic withdrawal plan (SWP) calculator models regular withdrawals from an invested corpus. It applies the assumed monthly return, subtracts the planned withdrawal and estimates the balance remaining at the end of the selected period.

How to use the SWP calculator

  1. Enter your starting investment corpus.
  2. Enter the planned monthly withdrawal.
  3. Set an assumed annual rate of return and withdrawal period.
  4. Review total withdrawals and estimated remaining corpus. Try lower returns or higher withdrawals to stress-test the plan.

Important limitations

The calculation assumes a steady return converted to a monthly rate. Real markets fluctuate; sequence of returns, taxes, fund expenses and withdrawal dates can materially change outcomes. A positive projected balance is not a guarantee that funds will last.

Frequently asked questions

Can an SWP exhaust my corpus?

Yes. If withdrawals exceed the portfolio's ability to sustain them, the balance can reach zero before the selected period ends.

Does the calculator include tax on withdrawals?

No. Tax treatment depends on the investment, holding period and applicable rules. Seek qualified tax advice.

How can I test withdrawal sustainability?

Try different return assumptions, withdrawal amounts and periods rather than relying on a single projection.
