Reducing-balance EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1]

P is principal, r monthly interest rate and n total monthly instalments. Lender fees, rate resets and rounding may change actual payments.

How a loan EMI calculator works

EMI means equated monthly instalment. For a reducing-balance loan, each instalment includes interest on the outstanding principal and repayment of part of the principal. As the balance falls, the interest portion generally falls too.

Formula used

For a monthly rate r and n instalments, EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is principal. When the rate is zero, EMI is principal divided by n.

Frequently asked questions

Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

Generally yes, but it usually increases total interest because the loan remains outstanding for longer.

Does this include processing fees?

No. Add lender processing fees, insurance and other charges separately when comparing offers.

Is the EMI exact?

It is an estimate. Lenders may apply daily interest, rate resets, fees, payment-date rules and rounding.
