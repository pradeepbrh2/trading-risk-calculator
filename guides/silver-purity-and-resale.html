Silver Purity and Resale Value: 999 vs 925

Silver products may be quoted at different fineness levels. The number describes the proportion of silver in the metal by mass, not a guaranteed resale price.

What do 999 and 925 mean?

999 fineness means 999 parts silver per 1,000 parts alloy (99.9%). 925 means 925 parts per 1,000 (92.5%), commonly called sterling silver. Check the product mark and documentation rather than relying on appearance.

Estimate fine-silver equivalent

For a simple estimate, fine-silver-equivalent weight = net metal weight × fineness ÷ 1,000. A 100 gram 925 item contains about 92.5 grams of fine-silver equivalent before deductions and non-silver components.

What affects resale payout?

Buyers may apply testing, refining, handling or market-spread deductions. Stones, solder, coatings and non-silver parts can affect accepted net weight. Retail making charges are not necessarily recoverable metal value.

Ask the buyer

  • Which purity test will be used?
  • Will gross weight or net silver weight be used?
  • Which reference rate and purity are applied?
  • What deductions or assay charges apply?
  • Will you receive a written weight and payout breakdown?
