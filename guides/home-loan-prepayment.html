Home Loan Prepayment: Reduce Tenure or EMI?

When you make an extra home-loan payment, your lender may recalculate the loan in different ways. Two common outcomes are a shorter remaining tenure with a similar EMI, or a lower EMI over a similar remaining tenure. Options depend on your loan agreement and lender process.

Reduce tenure

Keeping EMI broadly unchanged while reducing the remaining term can reduce interest because principal is repaid sooner. It may suit borrowers whose monthly cash flow is comfortable and who want to close the loan earlier.

Reduce EMI

Keeping the remaining tenure broadly unchanged while lowering EMI can improve monthly cash flow. The interest saving may differ from a tenure reduction under otherwise identical assumptions.

Before prepaying

  • Ask when the lender applies the payment and recalculates interest.
  • Confirm whether you can choose EMI reduction or tenure reduction.
  • Check minimum amounts, documentation and any applicable charges.
  • Keep an emergency reserve and consider higher-interest debt.
  • Review tax implications and the opportunity cost of using spare funds.
