Trading charges in India: what affects your net P&L?

A trade can be profitable before costs and much less profitable after costs. To estimate net P&L, traders need to understand fee categories that may apply to their broker, exchange, instrument and segment.

Common cost components

  • Brokerage: the broker's fee, often subject to a per-order or plan-specific structure.
  • STT: securities transaction tax, with applicability and rates depending on instrument and transaction side.
  • Exchange transaction charges: fees based on applicable turnover and segment.
  • Stamp duty: statutory charge with applicability depending on the transaction.
  • GST: may apply to specified service components such as brokerage and certain fees.
  • SEBI fees and other levies: statutory or regulatory charges where applicable.
  • Slippage: the difference between expected and actual execution price.

Why fixed online estimates can mislead

Rates can change and may differ by segment, instrument, order type, broker plan and current regulation. Use the current tariff published by your broker and verify actual charges against contract notes.

Gross versus net P&L

Gross P&L reflects the price movement and quantity. Net P&L subtracts applicable charges and estimated execution impact. For a round trip, remember that costs may apply to both entry and exit.
