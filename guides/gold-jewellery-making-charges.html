Gold Jewellery Making Charges: How to Compare a Bill

A jewellery quote is more than the gold rate multiplied by gross weight. Compare offers by separating gold value from non-gold components and charges, then compare the final payable amount for the same purity and design.

Confirm purity and rate basis

Confirm the quoted rate matches the purity: a 22K rate should not be compared directly with a 24K rate. Ask for the hallmark and purity details.

Check net gold weight

Ask the jeweller to separate net gold weight from stones, beads, enamel and other non-gold parts. Gross jewellery weight can overstate gold content.

Understand making and wastage

Making charges may be a percentage of gold value, a rupee amount per gram or a fixed amount. Ask for the calculation basis. Wastage may be quoted separately; compare what each percentage is applied to.

Request an itemised final quote

Ask for gold value, making charges, wastage, discount, other charges, tax and final payable amount. Tax treatment depends on the actual supply and invoice structure, so verify the current invoice and applicable official guidance rather than assuming tax applies only to a separately displayed line.

Before paying

  • Verify purity and rate per gram.
  • Separate net gold weight from stones/non-gold weight.
  • Confirm making charge and wastage basis.
  • Review discounts, tax and exchange/buyback terms.
  • Keep the itemised invoice.
