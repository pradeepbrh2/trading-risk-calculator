Privacy Policy

Calculator settings, saved instrument setups, charge assumptions, risk dashboard preferences, journal entries and the mini-game best score are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not automatically synced to our servers or another device.

Backups

You can export a backup from the Trade Journal. Anyone with access to your browser profile or exported backup may be able to view its contents. Clearing browser site data can erase locally stored records. Keep your own secure backup.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Some functionality may load third-party libraries from a content delivery network. Those providers may process technical request information under their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project's public issue tracker: GitHub Issues. Do not post passwords, account numbers or private financial information.
