Frequently asked questions

What is a step-up SIP?

A step-up SIP increases the regular contribution at a chosen interval, commonly once a year, by a fixed percentage or amount.

Are returns compounded monthly?

This calculator uses its stated monthly return assumption to project contributions. Actual mutual fund returns vary and do not arrive as a steady monthly rate.

What if I cannot increase my SIP every year?

The result is only a scenario. Choose an increase you can realistically sustain and review it when income or expenses change.
