How GST is handled

For a ready-made jewellery sale, CBIC's sectoral FAQ says GST is 3% of the total transaction value, whether making charges are shown separately or not. A separate job-work service on silver supplied by the customer has a different treatment; the calculator provides a 5% making-charge-only option for that scenario. Confirm the invoice classification with the jeweller.

This is a shopping estimate, not tax advice. GST rules and invoice treatment can depend on the transaction structure. Rates are editable where appropriate; no live rate feed or jeweller quote connection is used.

How to calculate silver jewellery cost

Multiply the silver rate per gram for the selected fineness by the net silver weight. Add making charges, wastage and other bill items, subtract discounts, and apply the tax treatment shown on the jeweller's invoice. The calculator uses manual rates; it does not fetch live market prices.

Understanding silver fineness

  • 999 fine silver: commonly described as 99.9% silver.
  • 925 sterling silver: commonly described as 92.5% silver.
  • 900 and 835: lower-fineness silver alloys; use the purity test and rate basis confirmed by the seller.

Frequently asked questions

Is the silver rate the same for 999 and 925?

No. The rate depends on fineness, market reference, product and seller. In Buy mode enter the rate matching the selected fineness; in Sell mode enter the 999 fine reference rate.

Are stones included in silver weight?

They should be excluded when the seller or buyer calculates net silver content separately. Enter the non-silver weight in the field provided.

Does the calculator fetch live silver prices?

No. Enter a current rate from a source you trust or the jeweller's quotation.
