Reducing-balance formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1]

P is principal, r is monthly interest rate and n is total monthly instalments. Lender fees, rate resets, insurance and rounding can change actual outgo.

How to estimate home loan EMI and interest

A home loan EMI estimate depends mainly on the loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment tenure. The calculator models a standard monthly reducing-balance repayment and displays the instalment, total interest and total repayment.

How to use the home loan calculator

  1. Enter the principal amount you plan to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual rate quoted by the lender.
  3. Choose the tenure in years.
  4. Compare the EMI and total interest across different rates and tenures.

Costs to check beyond EMI

Before accepting a home loan offer, check processing and legal fees, valuation charges, insurance, benchmark-linked rate resets, prepayment conditions and the lender's amortisation schedule. These are not all represented in this simple estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Will a lower interest rate always mean a lower total cost?

It generally reduces interest for the same principal and tenure, but compare fees, reset terms and the actual loan schedule too.

Does a longer tenure reduce the monthly EMI?

Usually yes, but it generally increases total interest paid.

Can I reduce interest through prepayment?

Part-prepayments may reduce tenure or EMI depending on lender rules. Use the loan prepayment calculator to model an illustrative scenario.
