CAGR Calculator

Measure the annualized compounded growth rate between two values. CAGR smooths the path and does not show volatility.

How it works

CAGR = (Ending value ÷ Starting value)^(1 ÷ years) − 1.

This calculator provides estimates for education and planning only. It does not predict investment performance or represent an offer or recommendation. Market returns, taxes, fees and product terms can change.
