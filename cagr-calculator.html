Frequently asked questions

What does CAGR tell me?

CAGR is the constant annual growth rate that would turn the starting value into the ending value over the selected period. It smooths the path and does not show volatility.

Does CAGR include deposits or withdrawals?

No. This simple CAGR calculator assumes one starting value and one ending value. For dated cash flows, use the XIRR calculator.

Can CAGR be negative?

Yes. If the ending value is below the starting value, CAGR is negative and represents an annualised decline.
