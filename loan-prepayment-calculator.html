Home Loan Prepayment Calculator

See how extra monthly payments or an annual lump sum could reduce your loan tenure and interest, compared with keeping the original EMI.

Calculation assumptions

Monthly interest = outstanding balance × (annual rate ÷ 12)

EMI is calculated using the reducing-balance formula and kept unchanged after prepayment. Extra monthly payment is applied after the regular EMI; annual lump sum is applied after every 12th monthly payment. The final instalment is capped at the remaining balance and interest.

Illustrative only. Actual lender treatment, prepayment limits or charges, rate resets, payment dates and tax effects may differ. Confirm your lender's rules before making a prepayment. No lender or loan account connection.

Frequently asked questions

Does prepayment always reduce EMI?

Not necessarily. Lenders may reduce tenure while keeping EMI similar, or reduce EMI while keeping tenure. Confirm the adjustment option with your lender.

Are prepayment charges included?

The calculator estimates interest and tenure impact from the entered assumptions. It does not automatically include lender fees, contractual restrictions or tax effects.

Why can actual savings differ?

Actual results depend on the lender's interest method, payment posting date, rate resets, EMI changes and prepayment allocation.
