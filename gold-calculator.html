Old gold exchange against new jewellery

Old gold value is treated as a payment/credit, not as a reduction of the new jewellery taxable value. Confirm the jeweller's invoice and exchange policy.

How GST is handled

For a ready-made jewellery sale, CBIC's sectoral FAQ says GST is 3% of the total transaction value, whether making charges are shown separately or not. A separate job-work service on gold supplied by the customer has a different treatment; the calculator provides a 5% making-only option for that scenario. Confirm the invoice classification with the jeweller.
