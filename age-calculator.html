Frequently asked questions

How is exact age calculated?

The calculator compares the date of birth with the selected as-of date and accounts for calendar month lengths and leap years. It reports completed years, months and days.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Choose any as-of date on or after the date of birth to calculate age on that date.

Why can age in days differ from years multiplied by 365?

Calendar years include leap years and months have different lengths, so a calendar-age calculation is not the same as dividing total days by 365.
