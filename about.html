About Trading Risk Calculator

Trading Risk Calculator is a free, browser-based toolkit built to help individual traders plan position size, understand planned risk and review completed trades. It focuses on practical calculations rather than stock tips or trading signals.

What you can do here

  • Estimate position quantity using a risk budget and stop-loss distance.
  • Review assumed buying power, planned margin and reward targets.
  • Track daily realised P&L and personal risk limits.
  • Record completed trades and review net P&L, estimated costs and performance patterns.
  • Estimate trading costs using rates you configure from your broker's current tariff.

How calculations work

Risk-based quantity is the risk budget divided by the absolute entry-to-stop distance, rounded down to whole units. The calculator also estimates buying-power quantity using the leverage assumption entered by the user. The lower quantity is shown as the planning limit.

Journal analytics use trades and cost estimates saved in your browser. These are user-entered records, not broker-verified statements.

Independence and limitations

This is an independent educational utility. It is not a broker, exchange, investment adviser or research analyst. It does not connect to trading accounts, fetch live market prices, place orders or guarantee outcomes. Calculations are estimates and should be checked before any trade.

Editorial approach

We aim to explain formulas, assumptions and limitations clearly. When a rate or rule can change, users should verify it against current official broker, exchange and regulator information rather than rely on hard-coded examples.
